Welcome to Website Time Tracker

Let's get you set up in under a minute. You can change all of this later in Settings.

1. How it works

  • Open the extension popup on a site and press Start Tracking to begin timing it.
  • The timer stops when you are idle, the browser loses focus, or you press Pause.
  • Mark sites as productive or unproductive to see your productivity ratio.
